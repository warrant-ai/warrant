# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build and argue about software. The arguments are welcome. The following are not, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented:

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning language about anyone, whether or not they are present.
- Harassment of any kind, including unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Discrimination or exclusion on the basis of who someone is.
- Sustained disruption, including repeatedly reopening a settled decision without new evidence.

Disagree with the work, in detail and in public. Do not make it about the person.

Reports go to the maintainers through a private security advisory at https://github.com/warrant-ai/warrant/security/advisories/new, which only maintainers can read, with "conduct" in the title. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules, with an explanation to the person concerned.

This applies to people and to agents acting on their behalf. A person is responsible for what their agent posts here.
